Welcome to my homepage! I am an incoming Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Singapore Management University (SMU), where I will be advised by Prof. Hady W. Lauw. I hold a B.S. in Computer Science (Honor Program) at the University of Information Technology (UIT), VNU-HCM, where I was advised by Dr. Thanh Duc Ngo.

Research

My research primarily lies at the intersection of Vision-Language Models (VLMs), Multi-modal Representation Learning, and Recommender Systems. I focus on developing highly discriminative retrieval systems and advancing fine-grained cross-modal alignment to optimize representation spaces.

In the long term, I aim to bridge the gap between rigorous academic research and scalable, user-centric AI applications, bringing world-class multimodal technologies to real-world products.
